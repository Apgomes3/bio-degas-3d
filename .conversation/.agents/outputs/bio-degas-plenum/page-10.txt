Symbol
Implemented equation
internalLength
externalLength − 2×wall
internalWidth
externalWidth − 2×wall
occupiedCrateLength
max(column+1) × 740
totalTrayLength
trayCount×3405 + max(0,trayCount−1)×100
occupiedEquipmentLength
max(occupiedCrateLength,totalTrayLength)
actualAccessLength
max(0, internalLength − occupiedEquipmentLength)

wall + actualAccessLength + max(0,(occupiedEquipmentLength−totalTrayLength)/2)
Fit and vertical equations
Check
Equation / rule
crateLengthCapacity
max(0, floor((internalLength−800) ÷ 740))
crateWidthCapacity
max(0, floor(internalWidth ÷ 1040))
verticalAvailable
internalHeight − 620 − trayGap
verticalCapacity
max(0, min(4, floor(verticalAvailable ÷ 530)))
capacity
crateLengthCapacity × effectiveRows × min(stack_height, verticalCapacity)
tray condition
internalWidth ≥ 2005 and internalLength−800 ≥ totalTrayLength
IMPORTANT
The nominal 800 mm access is a minimum deduction in capacity and tray fit. The visual actualAccessLength is 
recomputed from the occupied equipment footprint and may be larger.
