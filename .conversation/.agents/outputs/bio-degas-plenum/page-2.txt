EDP · Bio/Degas
This document translates the implemented Bio/Degas plenum designer into a self-contained 
arrangement and visualization contract. It is intended for engineering coordination and a separate 
3D project—not for fabrication release.
IN SCOPE
Media-driven crate quantity; recommended 
arrangement; tank/tray/access dimensions; top and 
longitudinal section semantics; validation; persistence 
and stale-result behavior; future 3D scene mapping.
OUT OF SCOPE
Structural calculations, FRP laminate schedule, 
hydrostatics, hydraulics, drainage/overflow, lifting, 
transport certification, fire/code approval and 
fabrication tolerances.
